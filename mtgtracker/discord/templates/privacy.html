Privacy Policy

Matt's Forge · Effective November 2, 2026

Matt's Forge is a free card game played as a Discord Activity. It's a hobby project run by one person ("we" below). This page says what we collect when you play, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • From Discord, when you sign in. The Activity asks Discord for three permissions: identify (your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar), guilds.members.read (used only to check whether you're a member of our home server, and if so, your nickname there), and rpc.voice.read (who in your voice channel is speaking, so the table can highlight them). We never receive or record audio. We don't get your email address, messages, friends list or payment details.
  • Your games. The deck you pick, the moves made at the table, the game log, the result and a replay. These records include your display name and may include your Discord user ID and a link to your avatar.
  • Decks you import. When you paste a deck link (for example from Moxfield or Archidekt), we fetch that public decklist and keep it so the deck can be played.
  • Lobby presence. Who's in an Activity and which deck they picked, held in memory for the few minutes before a game.
  • Server logs. Errors and sign-in steps, used to fix problems. Requests reach us through Discord's proxy, so we generally don't see your IP address.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and seat you at a table.
  • To show your name, avatar and speaking indicator to the other players at your table.
  • To keep game history and replays.
  • To find and fix bugs, and to stop abuse.

Who else sees it

  • Other players at your table see your name, avatar, deck and plays.
  • Discord runs the Activity and its proxy, under Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare carries traffic to our server, under its own privacy policy.
  • We fetch card data and images from Scryfall and decklists from the sites you link, from our server. We don't send them anything about you.
  • We'll disclose information if the law requires it. Otherwise we don't share or sell it.

How long we keep it

  • Lobby presence: a few minutes. Your sign-in session: up to 12 hours, held in the page, not in a cookie.
  • Game records, replays and imported decks: until they're deleted, so your history keeps working. Ask us and we'll delete yours.
  • Server logs: short-lived, and only for troubleshooting.

Your choices

  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email mtg.support@mattbonsall.com with your Discord user ID.
  • To stop sharing your Discord information with us, remove Matt's Forge under Discord's User Settings → Authorized Apps.

Children

You must be old enough to use Discord in your country (at least 13). Matt's Forge isn't directed at children. If you think a child under 13 has used it, email us and we'll delete their information.

Where it's stored

Our server is in the United States. By playing, you understand your information is processed there.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
